A cosy parcel-pushing puzzle.

Meet Bram, a post-mole with a satchel and a lot of parcels to deliver. Push every parcel onto a delivery bay. You can push one parcel at a time, and you can never pull. The rules fit in a sentence; the puzzles will keep you thinking.

How it works

Push. Never pull. Deliver every parcel.

Push one parcel

Swipe to walk. Walk into a parcel to push it one square. Two in a row won't budge.

Never pull

Push a parcel into a corner and it's stuck there. Think a few moves ahead, and undo is always free.

Fill every bay

When every parcel sits on a delivery bay, the room is done. Match par for three stars.

A new trick in every world

Each world teaches one new trick, then mixes it with the ones you know. The game gets harder as you go, and each world's toughest rooms are optional bonus deliveries. Every level is checked by our solver, and par is always the true shortest route, so three stars is always possible.

Questions

Good to know

When is Box Day out?

Soon. We'll announce the date here once it's set.

Which devices does Box Day run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 18 or later, and Mac with macOS 15 Sequoia or later.

Do I need to be online?

No. Box Day works fully offline. Only the optional Game Center features need a connection.

Is there a subscription, or ads?

Neither. Box Day is free to play with no ads, and Box Day Pro is a single, one-off purchase that unlocks everything for you and your family.

What data does Box Day collect?

None. Your progress stays on your device. See the Box Day privacy policy.
