Crosshue

A colour logic puzzle, a bit like a nonogram.

Every row and column gets one paint pot. Where they cross, the paints mix: red and yellow make orange, yellow and blue make green. Work out every pot from the swatches, by logic alone, and the print comes out perfect.

Pre-order on the App StoreHow it works

Pre-order now in the UK, US, Canada and Australia. Out on iPhone and iPad on 9 November; EU and Ireland coming soon. Free to play, no ads, no tracking.

How it works

Mix paint. Match every swatch.

One paint per line

Every row and column of the board gets one pot of paint, and pours it along the whole line.

Crossings mix

Where two lines cross, their paints mix like real paint.

  • +→orange
  • +→purple
  • +→green

Match every swatch

Pinned swatches show what each square must become. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, reachable by deduction alone. No guessing.

A new rule in every world

Locked pots, thinner that lightens, ink that darkens, split lines that pour two ways and premixed pots. Then come new kinds of clue: rings that name one paint in the mix, crossed-out misprints that rule a colour out, and twin and rival pots that must match or differ.

Questions

Good to know

Which devices does Crosshue run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.

Do I need to be online?

No. Crosshue works fully offline. Only the optional Game Center features need a connection.

Is there a subscription?

No. Crosshue is free to play, and Crosshue Pro is a single, one-off purchase that unlocks everything for you and your family.

What data does Crosshue collect?

None. Your progress stays on your device. See the Crosshue privacy policy.
